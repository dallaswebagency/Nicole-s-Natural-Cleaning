Service agreement

Residential & Office Cleaning Service Agreement

Welcome to Nicole's Natural Cleaning. The policies listed here serve to protect you, your pets, your belongings and our staff. When you agree to the initial quote, you agree to these policies.

Cancellations

Please give at least 24 hours notice so we can fill your spot. If it is less, there will be a $95 charge.

Schedule

Please try to be consistent with appointment times, as we may not be able to accommodate changes.

Access

Please ensure all access routes are safe and clear to pass, including driveways and walkways.

Locked out

If we are locked out on a scheduled cleaning day, there will be a $95 charge to cover travel and our time.

No refunds

We are professional and want to ensure everything gets done in a meticulous and detailed fashion. We are human and make mistakes, or Murphy's law takes over at times, but we try to be careful with your belongings. If there are any issues with the cleaning or you are not happy with the service, please let our staff know and we will try to help you out as much as we can.
